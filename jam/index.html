friendslop jam

One weekend. Build a multiplayer game on friendslop, by hand or with an AI tool, and play everyone else's on Sunday night.

  1. Get a theme

    Announced at the start. Expect something stupid.

  2. Build it

    The rooms, lobby and hosting are done, so the whole weekend goes into the game.

  3. Play them all together

    Every entry is a link. Everyone joins everyone's lobby, and the arguments decide the winner.

When

After the first public code drop, once the docs are good enough to build on. Watch the repo to hear about it first.
